# SAREMBOK VE — CONSTITUTION
## Foundational Rules

1. Sarembok is its own technology.
2. Sarembok must not become a wrapper around another AI provider.
3. External models and platforms are replaceable integrations.
4. Sarembok's core architecture must remain provider-agnostic.
5. Build original technology where it creates strategic differentiation.
6. Use mature open foundations where reinventing them would unnecessarily slow the platform.
7. Mobile users are first-class users.
8. MetaHuman is an interface/embodiment, not the platform itself.
9. Computer vision and OpenCV are first-class capabilities.
10. Multi-agent orchestration is a core capability.
11. Long-horizon execution, checkpointing, recovery and verification are core capabilities.
12. Security, identity and authorization are foundational.
13. Every major architectural decision is recorded.
14. Capability claims require evidence.
15. The long-term destination includes a Sarembok Computing Environment and Sarembok-owned kernel technology.
