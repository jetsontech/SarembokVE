Founder & development leadership

SarembokVE is developed and architected by the SarembokVE team, led by Tim Hall, Founder and AI Systems Architect. The team's work spans agent execution, distributed computing, persistent memory and developer-oriented software verification.

The project's public repository is maintained under the jetsontech GitHub account. Public engineering evidence is available there; proprietary enterprise components are not represented as open-source.
