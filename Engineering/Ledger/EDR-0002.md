# EDR-0002 — Provider-Neutral Intelligence
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Status:** ACCEPTED / FOUNDATIONAL

## Decision
External frontier models are replaceable providers behind Sarembok interfaces.

## Rationale
This prevents Sarembok from being architecturally coupled to a single model vendor and allows the platform to integrate multiple frontier and open technologies.

## Consequences
Model adapters, routing and capability contracts must remain provider-neutral.
