# EDR-0004 — Kernel Ownership
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Status:** APPROVED / LONG-TERM ROADMAP

## Decision
The long-term roadmap includes Sarembok-owned kernel technology. Existing operating-system foundations may be used during development.

## Rationale
Kernel ownership is part of the ultimate Sarembok computing-platform vision, but immediate progress should focus on the runtime, intelligence, agent and user-experience layers.

## Consequences
Kernel work is treated as a deliberate research program, not an immediate blocker for the platform.
