# EDR-0007 — Computer Vision
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Status:** APPROVED / ROADMAP

## Decision
Computer vision, including OpenCV-based pipelines, is a first-class Sarembok subsystem.

## Rationale
Sarembok must be able to perceive and reason about real-world and digital environments, not only process text.

## Consequences
Vision interfaces must support camera ingestion, multimodal context, perception results and secure downstream actions.
