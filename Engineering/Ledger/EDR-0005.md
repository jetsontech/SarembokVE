# EDR-0005 — MetaHuman Separation
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Status:** ACCEPTED / FOUNDATIONAL

## Decision
MetaHuman and Unreal are embodiment/client technologies and must not become the Sarembok platform boundary.

## Rationale
Sarembok intelligence must be reusable across desktop, mobile, web and future interfaces.

## Consequences
The MetaHuman layer consumes Sarembok services through stable interfaces rather than defining core platform architecture.
