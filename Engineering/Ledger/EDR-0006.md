# EDR-0006 — Mobile-First Reach
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Status:** ACCEPTED / FOUNDATIONAL

## Decision
Users with only Apple or Android devices are first-class Sarembok users.

## Rationale
The target platform is for the mass public and cannot assume ownership of a PC or laptop.

## Consequences
Cross-device identity, continuity, authentication, mobile UX and low-friction access are core architectural concerns.
